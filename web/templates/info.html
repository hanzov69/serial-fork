How to Request a Serial Number

  1. Join the Discord server using the invite link below.
  2. Complete your build and post about it in the designated build showcase forum channel.
  3. Copy the link to your forum post — it must be a discord.com link.
  4. In any bot-enabled channel, run /request, choose your printer type, and paste your forum post link.
  5. A moderator will review your submission. You will receive a direct message when it is approved or rejected.
  6. If rejected, you can update your post and re-submit using /resubmit or the My Requests page.

About

Thanks to the amazing Voron community, we have the concept of "Serial Numbers" for printer builds. But why should they have all the fun? Inspired by this, the BabyBelt / Crooked Crow community now has their own special way to do this. But with fancy websites and discord bots and stuff. If you have any issues, ping MrChristian on discord.
