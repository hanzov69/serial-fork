How to Request a Serial Number

  1. Join the Discord server using the invite link below.
  2. Complete your build and post about it in the build showcase forum channel. Make sure to include photos or videos of your build and apply the correct printer type tag to your post.
  3. Inside your forum post thread, run /request. The bot will detect your printer type automatically from the thread's tag.
  4. A moderator will review your submission. You will receive a direct message when it is approved or rejected, and a congratulations message will be posted in your thread.
  5. If rejected, you can update your post and re-submit using /resubmit inside the same thread, or from the My Requests page.

About

Thanks to the amazing Voron community, we have the concept of "Serial Numbers" for printer builds. But why should they have all the fun? Inspired by this, the BabyBelt / Crooked Crow community now has their own special way to do this. But with fancy websites and discord bots and stuff.

Everything is more fun with friends, so this software is available for anyone who may have a use for it. Feel free to implement it for your project, and PRs are always welcome.
